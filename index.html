🏛️ Zerodha Margin Trading Facility (MTF)

This analytical hub translates the complex institutional mechanics of Zerodha's Margin Trading Facility (MTF). MTF is a SEBI-regulated leverage mechanism allowing market participants to finance up to 5× leverage on over 1,500 Group 1 equity securities. While enhancing purchasing power, it introduces strict daily carrying costs (14.60% p.a.), automated Risk Management System (RMS) liquidations, depository pledge protocols, and dual-ledger accounting friction.

1. Capital Decomposition & Leverage Capital Balance

The gross invested cost is divided between Zerodha's funded loan and the client's initial margin:

Formula: Invested (I) = Funded Loan (F) + Initial Margin (IM)

₹5,95,000.00 = ₹3,90,000.00 (65.55%) + ₹2,05,000.00 (34.45%)

Leverage Multiple (L) = I ÷ IM

L = ₹5,95,000.00 ÷ ₹2,05,000.00 = 2.90× Purchasing Leverage

Proof that your initial margin is implicitly returned within the net sale proceeds upon position exit:

Identity: Net Credit = Present Value (PV) - Funded Loan (F)

💡 Real Statement Mechanics: Daily Posting & Weekend Catch-Up

Zerodha debits interest on the active funded value every calendar day, including weekends (Saturdays and Sundays) and exchange holidays:

  • Friday to Sunday Accrual: Interest for Friday, Saturday, and Sunday accrues independently and posts to your ledger on Monday as individual line items labeled Interest for MTF funded value on YYYY-MM-DD.
  • Formula: $\text{Daily Interest} = \text{Funded Loan Value } (F) \times 0.0004$. For example, a ₹3,125 funded balance yields an exact debit of ₹1.25 / day.
  • Allied Charges on Exit: When selling an MTF position, MTF unpledge charges (₹17.70 inclusive of 18% GST) and Depository Participant (DP) charges (₹15.34) are debited in the normal equity ledger.

🔍 Scrip Eligibility & Margin Formula Matrix

Margin requirements are not arbitrary; they are strictly determined by exchange statistical risk parameters ($\text{VaR}$ and $\text{ELM}$). For F&O segment stocks, the required initial margin is $\text{VaR} + 3\times\text{ELM}$. For Non-F&O stocks, it is $\text{VaR} + 5\times\text{ELM}$. Effective purchasing power leverage is $L = 1 / \text{Margin Requirement}$.

F&O Segment Securities Formula

Margin (%) = VaR + (3 × ELM)

Higher liquidity and derivative availability lower the extreme loss multiplier to 3×.

Non-F&O Segment Securities Formula

Margin (%) = VaR + (5 × ELM)

Cash-only securities carry elevated tail risk, requiring a 5× ELM buffer.

2. MTF Trades (Margin Funded) 2 Ledgers Split

The Split Rule: Zerodha operates TWO ledgers: Normal Ledger (Your cash wallet) and MTF Ledger (Your loan diary).

🟢 When you BUY (MTF):

Contract note says: ₹10,135.98. But your Normal Ledger only debits ₹35.98 (charges) + margin! The stock cost (₹10,100) sits in the MTF Ledger as loan.

🔴 When you SELL (MTF):

Contract note says Receivable: ₹6,954.05. But you DON'T receive ₹6,954 in your normal ledger because Zerodha takes back its loan first! You only receive: (Your Initial Margin) + (Profit/Loss) - (Charges).

🔒 Automated CDSL Depository Pledging Workflow

Under SEBI rules, all funded shares must be formally pledged to Zerodha Broking Limited – Client securities Margin Funding Account. With an active DDPI (Demat Debit and Pledge Instruction), this hypothecation happens automatically in the back-office pipeline, avoiding manual CDSL OTP verification. Positions carry an "M" tag on Kite.

⚠️ First-In, First-Out (FIFO) Tax Friction & Execution Locks

1. The FIFO Trap: Indian tax regulations enforce First-In, First-Out (FIFO) share reconciliation. If you hold 100 shares in CNC delivery (bought earlier at ₹1,500) and buy 100 additional shares via MTF at ₹1,200, an exit order intended for MTF will involuntarily sell your older CNC delivery shares first! This realizes an unintended capital loss on CNC while leaving the MTF loan active and accruing daily interest.

2. Order Lockout: If you hold an active CNC sell order, Kite will outright reject any fresh MTF buy order in the same scrip to prevent settlement netting shortfalls.

⚖️ SEBI Regulatory Framework & September 2024 Reform

Margin trading operates under the SEBI Master Circular for Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations, ensuring systemic stability and protecting clearing institutions from broker-level defaults.

SEBI CIRCULAR SEPT 2024

Circular SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2024/118 (Clause 4.3.3.5)

Issued in September 2024, this circular amended Para 4.3.3.1 of Chapter 1 of the Master Circular. Key provisions include:

  • Cash Collateral Maintenance: Funded Group 1 securities funded through cash collateral can now be considered toward the maintenance margin requirement.
  • Standardized Margin Standard: Funded Group 1 stocks used as maintenance margin require $\text{VaR} + (5 \times \text{ELM})$, regardless of whether the stock is in the F&O segment.
  • T+1 Upstream Reporting: Trading members must report daily MTF exposures to stock exchanges by 6:00 PM on $T+1$ day.
